Title: Executive Chef
Description:
Our award-winning fine dining restaurant is searching for an innovative Executive Chef to lead our culinary team. You will be responsible for designing seasonal menus, sourcing high-quality local ingredients, and ensuring all dishes meet our strict standards for taste and presentation. The role involves managing kitchen staff, maintaining inventory, controlling food costs, and ensuring strict adherence to health and safety regulations. You will work closely with the General Manager to create a cohesive dining experience.
Requirements:

Culinary degree or equivalent professional training.

5+ years of experience as a Head Chef or Executive Chef in a fine dining establishment.

Expertise in menu planning, recipe development, and food costing.

Strong leadership skills with the ability to manage a high-pressure kitchen environment.

Comprehensive knowledge of food safety and sanitation standards (ServSafe certified).
