Title: Digital Marketing Manager
Description:
We are a fast-growing retail brand looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to oversee our online presence and customer acquisition strategies. You will develop and execute comprehensive marketing campaigns across social media, email, and paid search channels. The role involves managing the advertising budget, collaborating with content creators, and analyzing campaign performance metrics to optimize ROI. You will be the voice of our brand online, driving engagement and building a loyal digital community.
Requirements:

4+ years of experience in digital marketing, social media management, or a similar role.

Proven track record of managing successful paid ad campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads).

Expertise in SEO, SEM, and content marketing strategies.

Experience with marketing analytics tools (Google Analytics, Hootsuite).

Exceptional copywriting and creative direction skills.
