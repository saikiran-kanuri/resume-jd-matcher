Title: Clinical Registered Nurse (ICU)
Description:
Our regional hospital is seeking a compassionate and highly skilled Registered Nurse to join our Intensive Care Unit. You will provide direct patient care, monitor vital signs, and administer medications for critically ill patients. The role requires rapid response to medical emergencies, operation of life-support equipment, and close collaboration with physicians and respiratory therapists. You will maintain detailed patient records and communicate complex medical information to patients' families with empathy and clarity.
Requirements:

Active Registered Nurse (RN) license in the state of practice.

Minimum of 2 years of clinical experience in an ICU or critical care setting.

Current BLS and ACLS certifications.

Deep understanding of pharmacology, anatomy, and critical care protocols.

Excellent physical stamina and the ability to work 12-hour shifts.
