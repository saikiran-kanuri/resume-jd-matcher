Title: Regional B2B Sales Executive
Description:
We are looking for a driven Regional Sales Executive to expand our market share in the commercial real estate sector. You will be responsible for building a robust sales pipeline, cold-calling prospective clients, and conducting on-site presentations. The successful candidate will negotiate complex contracts, close high-value deals, and maintain long-term relationships with key accounts. You will be expected to meet and exceed aggressive quarterly sales quotas while tracking all activities in our CRM system.
Requirements:

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, or a related field.

3+ years of proven experience in B2B sales or account management.

Strong negotiation, persuasion, and closing skills.

Experience using CRM software (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot).

Willingness to travel up to 50% of the time within the assigned territory.
