Title: Retail Store Manager
Description:
We are seeking an experienced Retail Store Manager to oversee daily operations at our flagship location. You will be responsible for driving sales performance, managing store inventory, and delivering an exceptional customer service experience. Duties include recruiting, training, and scheduling retail staff, as well as executing visual merchandising strategies to highlight seasonal product launches. You will handle customer escalations, manage the store budget, and ensure all loss prevention protocols are strictly followed.
Requirements:

High school diploma required; Associate’s or Bachelor’s degree preferred.

3+ years of retail management experience.

Strong leadership and team-building capabilities.

Experience with point-of-sale (POS) systems and inventory management.

Excellent interpersonal and customer service skills.
