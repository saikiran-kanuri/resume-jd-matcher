Microsoft Engineer (Fresher) – Job Description

Job Title: Microsoft Engineer – Fresher

Job Summary:

We are looking for a motivated and enthusiastic Microsoft Engineer (Fresher) to join our team. The candidate will assist in supporting, maintaining, and implementing Microsoft technologies such as Microsoft 365, Windows, Azure, and related services.

Key Responsibilities:

Provide basic technical support for Microsoft products and services.
Assist in the installation, configuration, and maintenance of Microsoft 365 applications.
Support user account management, email setup, and troubleshooting.
Monitor system performance and resolve basic technical issues.
Assist with Windows OS installation, updates, and maintenance.
Collaborate with senior engineers on IT projects and deployments.
Required Skills:

Basic knowledge of Microsoft 365, Windows OS, and networking.
Understanding of computer hardware and software troubleshooting.
Basic knowledge of Azure is an added advantage.
Educational Qualification:

B.E/B.Tech , Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
Preferred Skills:

Knowledge of Active Directory.
Familiarity with Microsoft Teams and Outlook.
Basic cloud computing knowledge.
Experience: Freshers (0–1 Year)
